Floe & Fish

Fish drop toward the sea, each carrying a key. Type the key and the seal on the ice floe catches the fish. Let five fish reach the water and the round ends. Each wave falls faster.

Keys

Words

Rest your fingers on the home row: A S D F for the left hand, J K L ; for the right. The glowing key on the board below is the next one to press. Esc pauses. In word lessons, Backspace lets go of a word you started.

This game is built for a physical keyboard. Without one, tap the sea during a round to open the on-screen keyboard.

Paused

Press Esc or Enter to keep going.
